## What 99.97% does and does not mean

Read this before quoting the number.

- **It covers 48.21% of formula cells.** 358,993 of 706,765 Excel-labeled cells read other workbooks. External
  links are a v1 non-goal, so these cells were not compared at all. The headline says nothing about them.
- **The corpus shaped the evaluator.** The first run scored 97.0166%, and fixes were made while looking at this
  exact corpus. A new corpus can be expected to find more. This is not a held-out result.
- **Three judgment calls moved the number, each with an ADR.** The first two together cover 10,000 of the 10,225
  mismatches in the first run:
  - 8,000 `XIRR` cells differ by under 1e-8 in absolute terms, which is the accuracy Microsoft documents. They
    now count as within tolerance (ADR-0015). Excel's solver stops earlier than ours, and neither is more right.
  - 2,000 `XIRR` and `XNPV` cells stored `#NAME?` because the Analysis ToolPak was not loaded when they were saved.
    They are not comparable, and leave the denominator rather than count as matches (ADR-0014).
  - The snap to zero is an estimate. 2^-48 added 7 mismatches in the lower-trust group, and 2^-50 removed them.
    This fits the corpus, but Excel's real rule is not documented, and no Excel was available to test (ADR-0016).
- **Most of those `XIRR` cells come from a few files.** 9 files account for all 9,000 calls, and these are
  variants of one template.
- **84,209 compared cells rest on a cached value upstream.** Those cells match partly because an input was taken
  from Excel, so they test less than cells calculated from scratch.
- **Agreement with cached values depends on them being current.** A workbook saved with manual calculation could
  store stale values. Some of the 89 mismatches may be this. It could not be checked without the cell contents.
- **164 of 616 Enron files were not read,** as in M1.
- **Enron's formulas are `.xls` formulas rendered by POI.** `.xlsx`-native behaviour can differ, so corpora are
  reported separately.

## What the 89 remaining mismatches are

They are in 13 files, and 28 are roots. The rest read a wrong cell. These stay as real mismatches.

- `REF-REF` and `REF+REF` where we give `#VALUE!` and Excel has a number: 9 roots. Text in an input that Excel
  coerces and we do not is likely, but this is unconfirmed because the cells cannot be published or inspected on CI.
- `AVERAGE(RANGE)` and `REF-AVERAGE(RANGE)`: 14 roots, 12 of them with small gaps. Excel's summation order or
  precision may differ. Unconfirmed.
- `IRR`: 2 roots, including one where we give `#NUM!` and Excel found an answer. This is a real solver gap.
- 3 other subtractions.

## POI's lower fidelity

POI's test data is a torture test and is not real-world usage (ADR-0008). 845 of its 853 mismatches are `TEXT`
with unusual formats. Its data is public, so CI uploads a per-cell
`latest-detail-poi.tsv` for it, never for Enron.

## What the corpus found

Plain bugs that unit tests written from documentation had missed, each now a case in `function-cases.tsv`:

- `ROUND` must work from 15 significant digits, so `=ROUND(0.25-0.2,1)` is 0.1. This cleared 57 Enron cells.
- `HOUR` and `MINUTE` returned fractions.
- `DATE(1900,1,22222)` was a day off from Excel's fictitious leap day.
- `T` of a range, `PRODUCT` with no numbers, `VARP` with no numbers, `LOG` error order, `FLOOR(0,0)`, `MODE` with text.
- `COUNTIF`-family criteria given as a range or an array constant.

Keeping public results to aggregates worked: patterns such as `ROUND(SUM(RANGE)/NUM,NUM) | number vs number, gap
below 50%` were enough to find the `ROUND` bug without publishing a cell.

## Known limits

- **`TEXT`:** no fractions, conditional sections, or elapsed time such as `[h]`. These give `#VALUE!` rather than a
  wrong answer. Dates are en-US only.
- **`SUBTOTAL`:** does not skip nested subtotals or hidden rows.
- **`LOOKUP` and `HLOOKUP`:** can differ from Excel on unsorted, mixed-type data.
- **Out of scope, reported as unsupported:** external, 3D, union, and structured references, and data tables.
- **Not implemented:** `HPVAL` and `HPHEA` (an HP add-in, so not plannable), `CELL`, `MDETERM`, `MINVERSE`, and others.
- **`--set`:** `eval` refuses to print a result that rests on a cached value after a change, rather than risk a stale
  answer. That includes anything reading a formula with an unsupported feature.
